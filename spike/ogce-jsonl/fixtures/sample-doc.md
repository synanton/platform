## Products

The Orion Sensor is a battery-powered environmental sensor. The Orion Sensor reports temperature, humidity, and vibration every sixty seconds. The Orion Sensor supports a ten-year battery life in normal operating conditions. Acme Corporation supplies the Orion Sensor to Northwind Logistics.

The Atlas Gateway is a rack-mounted aggregation gateway. The Atlas Gateway supports up to 64 connected field sensors. The Atlas Gateway exposes a local HTTP interface and a Modbus interface. Acme Corporation supplies the Atlas Gateway to Northwind Logistics.

The Vega Controller is a DIN-rail control unit. The Vega Controller connects legacy conveyor systems to a modern monitoring platform. The Vega Controller supports digital inputs, relay outputs, and an Ethernet uplink. Beta Ltd supplies the Vega Controller to Northwind Logistics.

The Nova Relay is a compact alarm-forwarding device. The Nova Relay forwards alarm events from the warehouse floor to the central operations desk. The Nova Relay supports redundant network paths. Beta Ltd supplies the Nova Relay to Northwind Logistics.

## Risks and recommendations

The review identified three risks. First, the Orion Sensor battery life depends on the reporting interval. A shorter interval would reduce battery life. Second, the Vega Controller firmware update process requires a maintenance window. An interrupted update could stop a conveyor line. Third, the Nova Relay depends on redundant network paths. A shared network outage could delay alarm delivery.

The review recommends three actions. First, Northwind Logistics should keep the Orion Sensor reporting interval at sixty seconds. Second, Beta Ltd should provide a rollback procedure for the Vega Controller firmware. Third, Northwind Logistics should test the Nova Relay during a planned network maintenance window. Sofia Almeida owns the first action. Daniel Okafor owns the second and third actions.

## Data-quality review

The pilot produced 1.2 million Atlas Gateway readings. The review team sampled 12,000 readings for completeness. The sample contained 11,940 complete readings and 60 incomplete readings. The incomplete readings were caused by a network interruption in the Leeds returns area.

The review team compared the sample against the warehouse management platform. The comparison found 18 timestamp mismatches. All 18 mismatches were within the same reporting minute. The review team classified the mismatches as benign clock skew.

The review team also reviewed the alarm events. The Nova Relay delivered 312 alarm events during the pilot. The review team confirmed 301 alarm events against the operations log. The remaining 11 alarm events were the false alarms recorded earlier. The review team closed nine false alarms and left two open for follow-up.
