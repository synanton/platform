Zephyr-9 Calibration Protocol (Revision B)

Quarterly torque audits of the auxiliary coolant manifold are mandatory for the
Zephyr-9 platform. Technicians must log manifold pressure drift, verify the
titanium retaining fasteners, and replace any fastener whose audit tolerance
exceeds two percent. The Zephyr-9 protocol uniquely requires a helium leak
check after every fifth audit cycle.
