# Globex Manufacturing - Supplier Profile

## Overview

Globex Manufacturing is a tier-one industrial supplier specializing in precision metal components, castings, and machined assemblies for the automotive, aerospace, and defense industries. The company was established in 1971 in Columbus, Ohio, and has since expanded to operate six production facilities across Ohio, Ontario (Canada), and Tennessee. Globex Manufacturing employs approximately 6,800 people and generated revenues of $1.4 billion in 2024.

## Products and Capabilities

Globex Manufacturing's core product lines include sand and die castings in aluminum and iron, closed-die forgings in steel and titanium alloys, CNC-machined housings and structural brackets, and weld assemblies for powertrain and chassis applications. The company operates 240 CNC machining centers and maintains a tooling division capable of designing and fabricating production tooling in-house, reducing lead times for new part launches.

## Key Customers

Globex Manufacturing serves a diversified customer base that includes several major original equipment manufacturers as well as mid-market distributors. **Acme Corp** is one of Globex Manufacturing's top five customers by revenue, representing approximately 14% of annual shipment volume. The relationship between Globex Manufacturing and Acme Corp is governed by a master supply agreement that includes capacity reservation commitments and pricing mechanisms tied to commodity indices.

## Quality and Certifications

Globex Manufacturing holds the following certifications across its facilities: IATF 16949:2016 (all automotive plants), AS9100D (Ohio and Tennessee aerospace lines), ISO 14001:2015 (environmental management), and NADCAP accreditation for special processes including heat treatment and non-destructive testing.

## Recent Developments

In Q3 2024, Globex Manufacturing announced a $95 million capital investment program to expand its Ontario facility and install a new high-pressure die casting cell targeting electric vehicle battery enclosure components. This expansion is expected to add 400 jobs and increase annual capacity by approximately 18% by mid-2026.
