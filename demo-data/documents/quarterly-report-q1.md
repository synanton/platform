# Acme Corp - Q1 2025 Quarterly Business Review

## Financial Performance

Acme Corp reported Q1 2025 revenues of $218 million, representing 5.2% growth year-over-year driven by strong demand in the aerospace aftermarket and a full quarter of shipments under the renewed supply agreement with Globex Manufacturing. Gross margin improved 80 basis points to 34.7% compared to Q1 2024, primarily due to commodity index price adjustments that reduced input costs for steel and aluminum castings.

EBITDA for the quarter was $28.4 million (13.0% margin), in line with management guidance. Free cash flow was $19.1 million, partially offset by a $6.3 million increase in safety stock inventory positions established to mitigate supply chain risk ahead of the anticipated peak production season in Q2 and Q3.

## Supply Chain Performance

| Globex Manufacturing | 97.3% | 42 | A |
| Initech Ltd | 98.1% | 18 | A+ |
| Other Tier-1 Suppliers (avg) | 93.6% | 97 | B+ |

Globex Manufacturing achieved its highest on-time delivery rate in four years, benefiting from the new production planning system deployed at its Ohio facility in late 2024. Initech Ltd continued to perform above contracted service levels, with zero critical escapes during the quarter.

## Operational Highlights

Acme Corp completed the qualification of a secondary casting supplier in Q1, reducing single-source exposure for three high-criticality part families previously sourced exclusively from Globex Manufacturing. The new supplier completed a successful first-article inspection in March and is expected to receive its first production purchase orders in Q2 2025.

The company also launched a digital supplier portal that enables Globex Manufacturing and Initech Ltd to submit capacity forecasts, quality documentation, and shipment confirmations directly into Acme Corp's ERP system, eliminating an estimated 340 manual data entry transactions per week.

## Outlook

Management reaffirms full-year 2025 revenue guidance of $870-890 million. Key risks to the outlook include potential softening in North American automotive production volumes and the pace of recovery from a Q4 2024 flooding event at one of Initech Ltd's component sub-suppliers in Poland.
