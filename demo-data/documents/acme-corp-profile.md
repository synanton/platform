# Acme Corp - Company Profile

## Overview

Acme Corp is a mid-sized industrial manufacturing and distribution company headquartered in Springfield, with regional offices in Denver, Atlanta, and Toronto. Founded in 1987, Acme Corp designs and distributes precision-engineered components used primarily in the automotive, aerospace, and heavy equipment sectors. The company employs approximately 4,200 people globally and reported annual revenues of $870 million in fiscal year 2024.

## Supply Chain and Key Suppliers

Acme Corp operates a tiered supplier network comprising over 340 active vendors across 18 countries. The company's two most significant manufacturing partners are **Globex Manufacturing** and **Initech Ltd**, which together account for approximately 62% of Acme Corp's inbound component volume.

- **Globex Manufacturing** supplies precision castings, forged steel blanks, and machined subassemblies from its facilities in Ohio and Ontario. The partnership has been in place since 2003 and includes a long-term supply agreement renewed in 2022 through 2027.
- **Initech Ltd** provides electrical harnesses, printed circuit board assemblies, and thermal management components. Initech Ltd operates three facilities that are ISO 9001:2015 and IATF 16949 certified, ensuring alignment with Acme Corp's quality standards.

## Strategic Priorities

Acme Corp's 2025-2027 strategic plan focuses on supply chain diversification, nearshoring of critical component manufacturing, and digitalization of procurement workflows. The company is actively evaluating additional suppliers in Mexico and Poland to reduce concentration risk associated with its current dependence on Globex Manufacturing and Initech Ltd.

## Risk Profile

Acme Corp's primary supply chain risks include single-region concentration with Globex Manufacturing, currency exposure related to Initech Ltd's Canadian and European operations, and increasing logistics costs driven by port congestion and fuel surcharges. The company's risk committee reviews these factors quarterly.
